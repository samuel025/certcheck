# Customer discovery

**Status: no interviews have been held. Nothing below is a finding.** Fill in the log only with real conversations. Do not describe these conversations as done until they are.

## Goal
Find out whether organizers or sponsors would pay for certificate issuance, outcome reports, or a programme plan, before building any payment feature. The current prices (0.00024 OKB per certificate after 25 free per 30 days; 0.12 OKB for a verified-issuer review) are hypotheses.

## Who to speak to (target: at least five)
Event organizers, bootcamp or school operators, community leads, and anyone who has had to report outcomes to a sponsor or funder.

## Questions
1. How do you issue certificates today, and who checks them?
2. After an event, what evidence do sponsors or funders ask for?
3. Show the report page (`#report`). What would you change before sending it to a sponsor?
4. Would you collect a wallet address from participants? What would stop you?
5. Would you pay for: certificate issuance? the outcome report? a programme plan with several cohorts? What range feels reasonable?
6. What is the biggest reason you would not use this?
