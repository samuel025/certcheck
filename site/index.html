See what the check does.

Every level asks one question: does your circuit give the right answer for every input? In the browser that is a truth-table comparison. From level 6 the same table is checked on X Layer: a contract runs your circuit on the processor and compares every row.

  • Levels 1–5: checked in your browser, no wallet
  • Levels 6–16: your circuit is taped out and checked on X Layer
  • The check itself is a free read; you pay only the tape-out fee

Questions you will ask

    

      
Who pays?
Sponsors would pay per verified graduate of their bounty level, or fund the faucet. A free pilot comes first. No sponsor has agreed yet, so this is a plan. Learners pay only gas and the 0.0013 OKB tape-out fee, and no BEM, because the faucet hands out NAND.

      
Why would a learner stay?
Each level unlocks the next. You earn stars for beating par, XP and ranks, a daily streak and badges. The last world is real FabX tasks instead of a certificate; on FabX, leaner circuits earn more $FAB (not guaranteed).

      
Is the content correct?
Every level's answer is a truth table. A solver proves each level is solvable at its par, and from level 6 the contract re-runs your circuit on the processor and compares every row. Levels 1–5 are checked in the browser only.

      
What is new here?
We have not compared against the leaderboard yet, so we make no claim of being first. What we can show is that the check is on-chain and the course ends in real tasks.

      
What can go wrong?
The contracts are small and unaudited. The faucet gives one claim per wallet and cannot stop one person using many wallets. Progress in the browser lives in local storage. By default circuits are minted on an existing free processor, not one we own.

Set up a wallet in two minutes

  
You only need a wallet to publish a batch. Checking certificates never needs one. A wallet is an app that holds a small balance and signs the one transaction that records your batch.

  
1
Install a wallet
Use a browser wallet such as OKX Wallet or MetaMask. Create a new account and write the recovery phrase on paper. Never type it into any website, including this one, and never share it.

  
2
Get a few cents of OKB
OKB is the coin that pays the tiny network fee. Publishing a batch costs a fraction of a cent, so a very small amount lasts a long time. You can buy OKB on an exchange and withdraw it to your wallet address on the X Layer network.

  
3
Publish
On the Issue page, press Connect wallet. The wallet will offer to add the X Layer network if it is missing. Approve it, then publish.
Go to Issue

  
Test your first batch with the sample list before using real names. A published batch cannot be edited, only revoked.
